# Bearings identity and project logos

The website's Bearings symbol is a new design proposal requested by the product owner on 19 November 2026. It uses the supplied CIRCL symbol as the recognizable red/slate aperture core, surrounded by a teal bearing/compass ring and six nodes representing the connected ecosystem. The nodes are symbolic, not a count or claim of operational integrations.

`bearings-mark-source.png` is the transparent high-resolution image generated with the supplied CIRCL symbol as a reference. The website serves a 256px WebP version and a 64px PNG favicon, encoded with ImageMagick; it does not load the high-resolution original. The generated interpretation is not an exact vector reproduction of the supplied mark. This is a Bearings identity proposal, not a replacement for CIRCL's official identity. The provided full `assets/circl.svg` remains unchanged in the operator footer, linked to CIRCL. `assets/circl-symbol.svg` retains the supplied symbol reference.

The nine other project identities are original assets obtained from their official repositories or websites. `../assets/projects/sources.json` records project names, original download URLs, website links, retrieval dates and content SHA-256 checksums. These logos are hosted locally, keep their original colors and aspect ratios, and link to the corresponding projects. No third-party image host is contacted by a page view. The surrounding integration descriptions remain authoritative: AIL is a manual configuration export, MISP is an optional bounded pilot with personal permissions, and native tool accounts/permissions remain independent.

CIRCL, NetBox, Vulnerability-Lookup, MOSP, MONARC, MISP, AIL, FlowIntel, RT and RTIR names/logos belong to their respective owners. Their inclusion identifies the operator and referenced projects, and does not imply additional partnerships or transfer trademark ownership. The repository's AGPL-3.0-or-later source license does not relicense third-party logos. Preserve applicable upstream notices and this source manifest when distributing the website. No new claim about official approval or a permissive trademark license is made.

## CIRCL corporate theme — 20 November 2026

At the product owner's request, the public marketing site, Bearings workspace/login and Django administration now follow the current visual language of [circl.lu](https://circl.lu/). The reference uses Raleway, white navigation, body `#23313a`, background `#f7f9fa`, blue `#315e73`, a `#173f52`–`#477a8f` introductory gradient and red `#de2d38` accents. The Bearings implementation is its own layout, with preserved content, semantic risk colours and the existing CIRCL-derived Bearings mark. No government crest or additional endorsement is introduced.

The unmodified regular Raleway font was retrieved from CIRCL's `/fonts/raleway-regular.woff` over verified HTTPS and is served locally for both standalone static pages and Django static files. `assets/fonts/` and `portal/static/portal/fonts/` retain SIL Open Font License 1.1, source URLs and font SHA-256 manifests. Keep these notices when distributing either artifact. Weight variants use normal browser synthesis as on the reference's regular-face setup; system Arial/sans-serif is the fallback. No Google Fonts or external font request is added. The existing mark is copied unmodified into the application's static brand directory for the workspace and admin headers.

The marketing overview image was refreshed from the retained **local synthetic NetBox lab company** in the new theme (1440 × 1653 viewport); it contains no operational company inventory or personal intelligence. Other historical synthetic workflow illustrations retain their documented illustrative purpose. Current public and workspace screenshots are also recorded under `docs/images/corporate-*`. Versioned stylesheet/updated preview links avoid showing a previously cached theme after deployment.
