What do the labels mean?
Source severity
The vulnerability publisher's technical severity; not this company's business risk.
Applicability
The analyst's assessment of whether the vulnerability applies to this installation.
Workflow
Where the review stands. Closing a review requires a reason; it does not independently prove a deployed fix.
Unknown
Information still needs to be checked. It does not mean safe.
