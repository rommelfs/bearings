Automatic Linux registration · {{ org.name }}

Approve a rollout once and distribute its limited key through your deployment secret manager. Each installed host generates its own device key and registers itself; repeat installation keeps the identity. No per-host public-key copy, NetBox entry or enrollment code is needed.

New hosts are recorded as self-reported observations in Bearings. Hostnames and IPs do not prove ownership and never attach a new key to an existing asset. Same-named machines remain distinct observations. The rollout key cannot read inventory, replace existing devices or grant upstream write access. NetBox stays under its current connection permissions.
