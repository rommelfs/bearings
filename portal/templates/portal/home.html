How review cues and access work

Supported distribution packages are checked automatically against vendor rules. A current affected-version match, an unresolved vendor rule and an out-of-date evaluation are separate states. Fixed/not-affected results retain their saved human workflow and history. This is an in-app review cue, not an email or proof of compromise. CVSS is source severity, not organizational risk. Unknown means missing source severity. High-criticality assets appear first within each severity group. Operator view: only organizations permitted by policy are included; sensitive operator reads are audited.
